# Final Intent Taxonomy — SpotifyCares Support Agent

## 1. Playback/Technical Bug
* **Definition:** Customer reports the app, a specific feature, local storage, or audio asset failing to perform as expected. This includes playback drops, hardware lock-screen freezes, local cache erasures, sync failures, or layout glitches.
* **Examples:**
  * *"Daily Mixes have not been refreshing... stop playing as soon as they hit the end."* (Tweet 3)
  * *"Issues with playback stopping when the Lock Screen activates."* (Tweet 7)
  * *"Android app occasionally wipes out every download... redownloading 1600 songs."* (Tweet 40)
  * *"Everything else works, it's just that the music won't play."* (Tweet 73)

## 2. Account Access
* **Definition:** Customer is locked out of their profile, trapped in authentication loops, unable to sign up for a new account, or facing delivery failures with system administration emails (like verification links or invitations).
* **Examples:**
  * *"Sent me an invitation for Spotify Family twice and I have yet to receive an email."* (Tweet 1)
  * *"System rejects my attempt to update my password for my locked out premium account."* (Tweet 74)
  * *"Why is it impossible for me to sign up to your website??"* (Tweet 70)
  * *"Deactivated Facebook and cannot log on to Spotify... What to do?"* (Tweet 80)

## 3. Billing/Subscription
* **Definition:** Customer reports transaction anomalies, incorrect recurring fees, promotion code redemption errors, or setup/verification bugs involving specific subscription tiers (especially Student Premium configurations via SheerID or third-party Hulu bundles).
* **Examples:**
  * *"You charged me twice for my service this month."* (Tweet 2)
  * *"SheerID is unable to verify my studentstatus. Enrolled in 2 universities."* (Tweet 8)
  * *"Seems that I get a receipt every 3 months and I am charged every month."* (Tweet 24)
  * *"Trying to activate my Hulu on Spotify for that student deal for almost an hour."* (Tweet 72)

## 4. Content Availability
* **Definition:** Customer reports a failure to locate a specific artist, track, album, or playlist due to explicit catalog gaps, region/licensing locks, or profile metadata contamination (e.g., songs misattributed to the wrong artist profile).
* **Examples:**
  * *"I fkn hate Spotify cause they don't have any [Artist Name]."* (Tweet 10)
  * *"This album is not available, @SpotifyCares ."* (Tweet 17)
  * *"There are tracks featured on my Spotify which I did not write."* (Tweet 45)
  * *"I hope rainz music gets added to spotify."* (Tweet 75)

## 5. Feature Request/Complaint
* **Definition:** Customer requests new capabilities, interface layout toggles, or structural modifications to platform rules (like changing download limits or blocking explicit audio). It also tracks functional frustrations with current default features, recommendation algorithms, or non-premium ad experiences.
* **Examples:**
  * *"I really want to be able to delete radio stations from my recently played."* (Tweet 11)
  * *"Can you please stop trying to shove Post Malone down my throat please?"* (Tweet 25)
  * *"WHY AM I GETTING BABY JOGGER ADS??! I DO NOT WANT BABIES MAKE IT STOP."* (Tweet 35)
  * *"Why is [Artist] cut out of hootie and the blowfish's profile pic?"* (Tweet 77)

## 6. Service Outage
* **Definition:** Customer queries whether the platform is experiencing a global or regional infrastructure crash, often explicitly noting that multiple distinct users or geographically separated friends are experiencing simultaneous service failures.
* **Examples:**
  * *"It seems that i can't play any song... Lot's of people are talking about this bug."* (Tweet 5)
  * *"Apparently it's not only me—asked the bf an hour away, and it goes silent for him too."* (Tweet 16)
  * *"Is Spotify down or is my phone being stupid???"* (Tweet 78)

## 7. Other
* **Definition:** Messages containing no technical diagnostic variables, account questions, or functional feedback. This encompasses raw conversational pleasantries, user status updates, channel transition handshakes, and pure sarcastic/social banter.
* **Examples:**
  * *"IT WAS A MISTAKE."* (Tweet 6)
  * *"DM sent, thanks."* (Tweet 13)
  * *"Are you hiring? Do I have to apply for a software developer position... to get this done?"* (Tweet 59)
